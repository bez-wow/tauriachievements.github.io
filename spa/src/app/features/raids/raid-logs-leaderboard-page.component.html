How is the score calculated?

On each boss, the character's best {{ metric().toUpperCase() }} is compared to the best {{ metric().toUpperCase() }} anyone did on that boss. These relative performances are added together and averaged. The average then sets the score, out of an arbitrary maximum of {{ maxScore }}: the character's score is the maximum score times their performance.

Example: character's DPS on the boss / best DPS on the boss = relative performance

  1. Boss 110 / 10010%
  2. Boss 2160 / 20080%
  3. Boss 3300 / 100030%
  4. Boss 40 / 10000%

Overall performance: (10 + 80 + 30 + 0) / 4 = 30%

Score: {{ maxScore }} × 0.3 = 2090.7, rounded down to 2090

The example raid has only four bosses, and the character didn't kill the last one.

Why not use average DPS/HPS?

With a plain average, the bosses where very high DPS or HPS is possible would count for more of a character's overall performance. Comparing each boss to its own best makes every boss count the same.

On WotLK's Flame Leviathan, for example, you can do 300k+ DPS, while on most other bosses it's under 10k. With average DPS, doing really well on Flame Leviathan would be enough and the other bosses would hardly matter. Other raids have the same problem, to a lesser degree.
